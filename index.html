Mulligans
  • Everyone starts with 1 mulligan per hole.
  • Outright win: the winner gets 0 on the next hole. Everyone else keeps 1.
  • Everyone ties: the skin carries and everyone gets 1 on the next hole.
  • Some players tie for low: the skin carries. Tied players get 1, anyone who wasn't in the tie gets 2 on the next hole.
